Thank you for helping. Issues and pull requests are welcome.

also be offered under other terms in the future (for example a commercial license or paid
features), every contribution is accepted on these terms:

1. You wrote the contribution yourself, or otherwise have the right to submit it.
2. You license your contribution to the public under the AGPL 3.0, like the rest of the

3. You also grant Laouen, the copyright holder of Super PM, a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, change, sublicense and distribute
   your contribution under any terms, including terms other than the AGPL 3.0.
4. You keep the copyright in your contribution.

You confirm this by adding the following line to each commit message (`git commit -s` does it
for you):

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>

and by stating in your pull request that you agree to this contributor agreement.

## Before you open a pull request

- Read `docs/` for how the scheduling engine and the app are organised.
- Add or update tests for what you change; run the tests as described in the README.
